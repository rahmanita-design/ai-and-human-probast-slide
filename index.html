03 / THE FRAMEWORK
4 DOMAINS OF INTEGRITY

The Four Pillars of Algorithmic Integrity

A unified audit standard applying equally to traditional regression and modern deep learning.

DOMAIN 01
Participants & Data Sources
Ensures study design and participant inclusion yield a representative dataset matching the intended target population.
DOMAIN 02
Predictors (Features)
Requires consistent definition, blinded measurement without knowledge of outcome data, and strict prevention of data leakage.
DOMAIN 03
Outcome Determination
Verifies objective, standard outcome definitions assessed independently without predictor data contamination.
DOMAIN 04
Analysis & Resampling
Audits sample size adequacy, continuous feature handling, class imbalance recalibration, and calibration vs discrimination curves.

Human Equity & Algorithmic Bias

Medical prediction models must not perpetuate systemic health disparities. PROBAST+AI embeds fairness assessments into every domain—demanding data diversity across age, sex, ethnicity, and geography.
